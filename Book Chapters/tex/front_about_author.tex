Sai Dheeraj Gummadi is a Lead Data Scientist with over five years of experience across startups and Fortune 500 companies, including FactSet, Motorola Solutions, and HighRadius. His work spans fintech and financial operations, from robust ETL pipelines and machine-learning systems to scalable microservice architectures. He focuses on bridging theoretical ideas with systems that can operate reliably in production.

Dheeraj holds a master's degree in data science from IU International University of Applied Sciences, Germany. He has published more than ten research papers in venues including IEEE, Springer, and Scopus-indexed publications, and holds patents with the United States Patent and Trademark Office and the Indian Patent Office. His writing and research aim to make sophisticated AI and economics concepts accessible to a wider technical audience.
